[Autonomous Research Result: Coordinate Systems K and K']
In Einstein's relativity, coordinate systems are represented by the letters K and K'. 
Frame K is the stationary system, or the reference frame at rest.
Frame K' is the moving system, which moves at a constant velocity relative to K.
The relationship between K and K' is governed by the Lorentz Transformation, not the classical Galilean transformation.
The principle of relativity states that the laws of nature are the same whether referred to coordinate system K or to coordinate system K'.
Measurements in K are (x, y, z, t) and in K' they are (x', y', z', t').
This leads to phenomena like time dilation and length contraction as objects move between coordinate systems.
The speed of light remains constant (c) in both system K and system K'.
